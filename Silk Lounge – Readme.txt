Silk Lounge – Readme

Game Name: Silk Lounge

Game Overview
You play as a spider swinging through a city filled with coins to collect and enemies that want to knock you off your web. Your goal in each room is to survive, collect coins, and clear enemies while using your web to swing across the level.

Controls:
Default controls (keyboard + mouse)
A / D: Move left and right
W / S: Climb up and down the slung web
Q: Shoot
Left Mouse Button: Swing on your web (When mousing over wall)
Alternate controls
Arrow keys: Move
E: Shoot
Right Mouse Button: Shoot
Pause / exit options are available through the in game UI (Esc where applicable).


How to Play:
Move along the web using A, D, W, and S.
Use Left Click to start swinging from your anchor point and build momentum.
Tap Q to shoot at enemies that patrol or chase you.
Collect coins scattered through the level.
Avoid touching enemies or hazards or you will take damage or lose progress in the room.
Collecting coins will also charge your Super Mode (see below).


Super Mode and Particle Effects:
When you collect 12 coins, your Super Mode activates:
Your shots deal increased damage.
A distinct particle effect plays on the player so it is clear that Super Mode is active.
Super Mode lasts for as long as you have 12+ coins, after which your damage will return to normal and the particle effects stop.


Cheat Codes:
Use these only for testing or debugging. Hold Tab, then press the key listed:
Tab + N: Skip to the next room
Tab + R: Restart the current room
Tab + C: Give the player 12+ coins (enough to trigger Super Mode)
Tab + Esc: Immediately end the game


Graduate Student Features:
a) Smart enemy behavior and paths
Enemies are placed on paths and use logic to chase the player instead of just moving in a simple pattern.
When you come into range, these “smart” enemies will adjust their movement to follow you, making positioning and swinging more important.

b) Particle effects and Super Mode
Super Mode uses a dedicated particle system on the player character to visually communicate the power up state.
The particle effects are triggered when the 12 coin threshold is reached and are turned off when the power up ends.
This combines game feel (visual feedback and speed change) with gameplay impact (higher damage and faster movement), satisfying the graduate level requirement for additional particle effects and more advanced behavior.


That is everything you need to know to start swinging through the Silk Lounge. Enjoy the game!
